# Leadership Update — Windows 11 Migration: End-User Experience
**Prepared:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** Non-technical management

---

The Windows 11 migration has broadly landed well for our users. The majority of the issues raised in the first week — including problems accessing files in OneDrive, inability to reach the shared Finance drive, VPN instability, and slowness logging into remote desktops — have all been resolved and are no longer appearing in user feedback. Satisfaction comments have increased week-on-week, and several staff have specifically noted the improvement. The overall picture is a migration that caused short-term disruption, as expected, and has stabilised well across most of the user base.

Two issues remain open and are being actively worked. First, the Floor 3 printer has been unavailable for two weeks running; the team has escalated this and a fix is in progress, but it continues to affect staff who need to print client documents from that floor. Second, a routine security update applied to Finance-team devices on 4 August introduced an unintended side-effect that significantly slows down the time it takes a device to become ready for use in the morning — adding roughly 24 seconds to every login. This does not affect any other team. We have identified the likely cause within the update and are working to correct it without removing the security controls it was intended to put in place. Both issues have owners, timelines are being set, and we will provide a further update once resolved.
