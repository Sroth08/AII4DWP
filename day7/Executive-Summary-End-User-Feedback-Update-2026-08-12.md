The migration is landing better for most end users than it did at the start. Feedback now shows that many of the early disruptions have settled down, day-to-day work is smoother for a broad part of the business, and confidence is improving. At the same time, user comments make clear that the experience is not yet consistent for everyone, so this should be treated as a steady recovery phase rather than a fully closed program.

What remains open are a small number of high-impact access problems that can still stop people from working, plus a few recurring service pain points in specific areas. We have already issued proactive staff guidance so people can try safe self-help steps before raising tickets, and support teams are actively triaging and escalating unresolved cases to the right owners. We will continue to provide regular leadership and staff updates on progress, focus first on work-stopping issues, and confirm fixes only after users report stable service.
