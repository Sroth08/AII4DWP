Close all work apps, then sign out of Windows and sign back in once. After signing in, open the vault first before opening other tools and check if you can access it.

If it still does not open, restart your laptop and try again on a different network if possible (for example, office network or phone hotspot) to rule out a temporary connection issue.

If you still cannot access it after these two tries, contact the Service Desk. Share: your username, device name, exact time of the last failed attempt, the vault error message text (or a screenshot), and whether it failed on more than one network. This will help them resolve it faster.
