# Self-Service Answers for Top 3 Themes

## AVD sign-in failures and account lockouts
Please try these steps:
1. Close the AVD app or browser window fully, wait 30 seconds, then sign in again using your full work email.
2. If it still fails, use the "Forgot password" option once, then wait 10 minutes before trying again so your account can unlock.

If you still cannot sign in after these steps, contact the Service Desk and share: the exact error message shown, the time of your last failed attempt, your username, and whether you tried app or browser.

## Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance)
Please try these steps:
1. Disconnect and reconnect to VPN, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. If it still says access denied, restart your laptop once and retry S: after login.

If access is still blocked, contact the Service Desk and share: screenshot of the error, drive name (S:/Finance), your department/team, and the time you last tested.

## OneDrive missing files and sync uncertainty
Please try these steps:
1. Open OneDrive from the taskbar, click "Resume syncing" if paused, then wait 3 to 5 minutes.
2. Sign in to OneDrive on the web and check if the missing file is there; also check the Recycle Bin.

If files are still missing, contact the Service Desk and share: file/folder names, last time you saw them, whether they are missing on both laptop and web, and a screenshot of any OneDrive warning icon.
