Subject: Update on Credentials Vault Access

Team,

We are aware that some staff are unable to access the credentials vault after the Windows 11 migration. This is a known issue and our support teams are actively working on it now.

While we work on this, please try these steps:
1. Close all work apps, sign out of Windows, then sign back in. Open the vault first before other tools.
2. If it still does not open, restart your laptop and try again on a different network if possible (for example, office network or phone hotspot).

If you still cannot access the vault after these two tries, contact the Service Desk and share: your username, device name, exact time of the last failed attempt, the vault error message text (or a screenshot), and whether it failed on more than one network.

Please stay calm, we know this is frustrating and we are treating it as a priority. We will post the next update by 4:00 PM today, even if the fix is still in progress.
