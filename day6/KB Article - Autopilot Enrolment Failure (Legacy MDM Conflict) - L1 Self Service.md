# Trouble Finishing Setup on Your New or Reset Device

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

## What's happening?

Some devices get stuck or show an error message while going through the automatic setup when they're first turned on, or after a reset. This is a known issue and our support team is already aware and working on it.

## Is my data safe?

**Yes.** This happens before your device is fully set up, so no personal files, emails, or saved work are affected. You will not lose anything by following the steps below.

## What you can try yourself

1. **Wait a few minutes.** Setup can take longer than expected — leave the device alone for 5–10 minutes before assuming something's wrong.
2. **Check your Wi-Fi or internet cable.** Your internet connection cutting out can interrupt setup — make sure you're connected and try reconnecting if needed.
3. **Restart the device and let it try again.** If setup seems stuck or shows an error, turn the device off and back on, then wait for it to try once more.
4. **Note down or photograph any error message.** If you see a code or message on the screen, write it down or take a photo so the Service Desk can help you faster.

## What NOT to do

- Don't try to change any settings, accounts, or anything technical yourself.
- Don't keep restarting more than two or three times — this won't speed things up.
- Don't worry about losing information — nothing is saved to this device yet at this stage.

## When to contact the Service Desk

Please contact the Service Desk if:

- Setup still doesn't complete after waiting, checking your connection, and restarting once, **or**
- You see a message saying the device is "already registered" or similar, **or**
- You're not sure what to do next.

## What to tell the Service Desk

To help us assist you faster, please have the following ready:

- Your name and department
- Whether this is a new device or one that was just reset
- Roughly what time you started setup
- The exact wording of any error message or code shown (or a photo)
- Whether you're connected by Wi-Fi or cable, and the network name if known
- Any ID number on a sticker on the device, if it has one

We're sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while this is resolved.
