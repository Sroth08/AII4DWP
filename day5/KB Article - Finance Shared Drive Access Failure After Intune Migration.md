# Trouble Accessing Your Finance Shared Folder

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 07/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

## What's happening?

Some Finance team members can't currently see or open their shared Finance folder (sometimes called your "drive") on their computer. This started after a recent update to how computers connect to shared folders. Our support team already knows about this and is working on a fix.

## Is my data safe?

**Yes.** Nothing has been deleted, moved, or changed. Your files are exactly where they were before — you simply can't reach them from your computer right now. Once the fix is applied, everything will look normal again.

## What you can try yourself

1. **Save any open work first.**
2. **Restart your computer fully** (not just lock or sleep it), then log back in.
3. **Wait a few minutes after restarting.** The connection to your shared folder can take a little while to reappear.
4. **Check if the folder has come back.** Open the folder icon you use to browse your files (usually pinned to your taskbar) and look at the main list of drives and folders on your computer for your Finance folder.

## What NOT to do

- Don't try to set the folder up again yourself — this needs to be fixed by our support team.
- Don't restart your computer more than once or twice — if it hasn't come back after that, contact the Service Desk instead.
- Don't worry about your files — nothing has been lost and there's nothing you need to back up yourself.

## When to contact the Service Desk

Please contact the Service Desk if:

- Your Finance shared folder is still missing after restarting your computer, **or**
- You urgently need a file from the folder and can't wait for the fix, **or**
- You see any error message when trying to open the folder.

## What to tell the Service Desk

To help us assist you faster, please have the following ready when you call or report the issue:

- Your name and department
- The name of your computer (check the label stuck on the device — the Service Desk can also look this up for you if you can't find it)
- Roughly what time you first noticed the folder was missing
- Whether you restarted your computer and what happened afterwards
- Any error message you saw (if any)
- Whether you need a specific file urgently

We're sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while this is resolved.
