# Black Screen After Logging In to Your Online Desktop

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 07/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

## What's happening?

Some people are seeing a black screen after logging in to their online desktop. This is a known issue and our support team is already aware and working on it.

## Is my data safe?

**Yes.** Your files and saved work are stored safely and are not affected by this issue. You will not lose anything by following the steps below.

## What you can try yourself

1. **Wait about 30–60 seconds.** For many people the screen clears on its own shortly after login.
2. **Sign out and log back in.** If the screen is still black after a minute, sign out completely and try logging in again.
3. **Restart your computer or laptop (not just the online desktop).** If it still doesn't work, restart the computer or laptop you're using to connect, then try again.
4. **Save your work first, if you can.** If you can see any part of your screen or app, save any open documents before signing out, just to be safe.

## What NOT to do

- Don't keep repeatedly logging in and out more than two or three times — this won't fix it faster.
- Don't try to change any settings, reinstall anything, or contact anyone outside the Service Desk about this.
- Don't worry about losing work — most programs (like Word and Excel) often save a backup copy of your work for you, which you can get back when you log in again.

## When to contact the Service Desk

Please contact the Service Desk if:

- The black screen doesn't clear after waiting a minute and trying to log in again, **or**
- You keep getting the black screen every time you log in, **or**
- You're worried you may have lost unsaved work.

## What to tell the Service Desk

To help us assist you faster, please have the following ready when you call or report the issue:

- Your name and department
- Roughly what time you first saw the black screen
- Whether the screen ever clears, or stays black the whole time
- Whether you had any unsaved work open at the time
- Any error message you saw (if any)
- The name of the computer or laptop you're connecting from

We're sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while this is resolved.
