# Floor 6 Incident Update

## What Happened
On Monday morning, staff on Floor 6 in the Legal area reported three main problems: some people could not sign in, some people waited much longer than normal to sign in, and some people who did sign in experienced very slow computer performance.

This affected daily legal work and reduced productivity at the start of the day. Based on reports received, the impact involved a notable share of the Floor 6 population, not a single person or isolated computer.

---

## What We Have Confirmed
Confirmed facts:
- Floor 6 users reported sign-in failures, long sign-in delays, and poor computer performance.
- A recent software change had been made for Floor 6 shortly before the Monday morning disruption.
- The first evidence set reviewed does not prove that this recent software change directly caused the disruption.
- One collected evidence set came from a machine type that does not match the affected staff computer profile, so that evidence set cannot be treated as final proof of cause.

Not yet confirmed:
- A single proven cause that explains all reported user impact.
- Whether every affected user followed the same failure pattern.

---

## What We Are Doing
We have already taken immediate containment actions to reduce further disruption while keeping decision quality high.

Actions already taken and underway:
- Limited further spread of the issue within the affected Floor 6 population.
- Applied targeted controls to reduce additional user impact while review continues.
- Preserved pre-change and post-change records so decisions remain defensible.
- Continued focused validation using information from the correct affected user environment.

We are continuing active oversight of user impact patterns and the effect of containment actions.

---

## What Remains Under Review
The following points remain under review:
- Whether the recent software change is a direct cause or only close in timing.
- Whether all impacted users share the same underlying problem path.
- Whether additional targeted actions are needed after current containment effects are confirmed.

These points are being validated before any final attribution statement is made.

---

## What Partners Should Expect
You should expect continued concise updates as containment effects are confirmed and as evidence from the correct affected environment is validated.

If users continue to have sign-in or severe performance problems, they should contact the Service Desk and provide their computer name and the time the issue occurred.

Current business risk remains high due to active disruption to legal work, but actions are already in place to reduce additional spread while review continues.
