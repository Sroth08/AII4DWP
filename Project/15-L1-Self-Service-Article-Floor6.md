## Problem
Some people on Floor 6 may have trouble signing in, may wait a long time to sign in, or may notice their computer is running slowly after sign in.

## What You May Notice
- Sign in does not complete.
- Sign in takes much longer than normal.
- Your computer feels slow after you reach your desktop.

## What You Should Do
- If you are already signed in, save your work often.
- If you still cannot sign in, contact support and share your device name and the time the issue started.
- If your computer remains very slow, contact support with the same details.
- Avoid repeated restart attempts unless support asks you to do so.

## When To Contact Support
Contact support right away if:
- You cannot sign in.
- Sign in continues to be very slow.
- Your computer stays slow and affects your work.
